About

I am a Data Scientist at Elexon, where I work with the data and analytics behind the GB flexibility markets. I also hold an honorary lectureship at the University of Essex.

My work combines optimisation, reinforcement learning and machine learning with the economics of energy markets: pricing in nonconvex markets, locational and national pricing, flexibility, and forecasting.

Before joining Elexon, I was a Lecturer in Data Science and Operational Research at Essex. Earlier, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Technical University of Munich with Martin Bichler, working on electricity market design. I hold a PhD in Computer Science from the Australian National University and CSIRO-Data61, supervised by Pascal Van Hentenryck and Gerardo Berbeglia. I also have a degree in Mathematical Engineering from the University of Chile.
